# Limitations

These limitations apply to every number and recommendation produced by BoponX and are also returned in the API (`limitations` field) and shown in the UI and printed 90-day plan.

1. **Grid resolution (`0.5° × 0.625°`, ~50 km)**: NASA POWER's daily precipitation (`PRECTOTCORR`) and temperature (`T2M_MAX`, `T2M_MIN`) come from gridded reanalysis and satellite products (MERRA-2 / GPM). Bangladesh's 64 districts fall into **40** distinct POWER grid cells, so neighbouring districts (for example Rajshahi and Natore, or Dhaka, Narayanganj, and Munshiganj) share the exact same cell. The API lists these in `shared_with` and the UI displays `"Same NASA grid cell as ..."`. A rain gauge on a specific farm plot can differ substantially from the cell average.
2. **Gridded drizzle vs. station gauge threshold bias**: Because a $50\text{ km} \times 60\text{ km}$ reanalysis grid cell averages rainfall over a large area, convective storms often appear as smoothed $2\text{–}6\text{ mm/day}$ grid-cell rainfall across multiple consecutive days. As a result, a cumulative threshold ($20\text{ mm}$ over $7\text{ days}$ with $>1\text{ mm}$ follow-up) on gridded data can trigger earlier in May than a point rain gauge at a single BMD weather station.
3. **Pre-monsoon (*Kalbaishakhi*) sensitivity in northeast & coastal districts**: While the `dry_spell_max = 7` follow-up rule filters out isolated April–May storms in western and central Bangladesh (such as the Barind Tract), regions with heavy, continuous May pre-monsoon rainfall (notably the Sylhet/Sunamganj Haor basin and southeastern Chattogram hills) can satisfy the 7-day onset rule in early-to-mid May. BoponX exposes the full sensitivity grid (including a `01 June` search start and `25 mm` threshold) in `/advisory` so advisers can inspect how much the onset estimate depends on the start date.
4. **Short record (`~25` years)**: NASA POWER daily data in this configuration starts in `2001`. Splitting into `2001–2012` (at most 12 seasons) and `2013–latest` (at most 13–14 seasons) gives a two-sample test with modest statistical power: small shifts (`< 5 days`) cannot be distinguished from year-to-year monsoon variability, and 95% confidence intervals are wide.
5. **Definition sensitivity**: There is no single universally agreed numerical definition of monsoon onset for planting. BoponX therefore computes and returns a sensitivity grid across thresholds (`15, 20, 25 mm`), window lengths (`5, 7, 10 days`), dry-spell lengths (`5, 7, 10 days`) and search-start dates (`15 Apr`, `01 May`, `01 Jun`). Where the shift changes sign or magnitude across definitions, the user should treat the point estimate with caution.
6. **Onset shift ≠ optimal transplanting shift**: Moving the BAMIS planting window by the same number of days as the rainfall-onset shift is a **planning rule (`rule_based`)**, not an empirically calibrated crop-response model. Standing water depth, cultivar duration, seedbed age, canal water release, and labour availability all affect the real transplanting date.
7. **Latency, not a forecast**: NASA POWER daily values lag real time by roughly a week. BoponX is historical and near-real-time agroclimatic context; it is **not** a weather forecast. Short-range operational decisions should check Bangladesh Meteorological Department (BMD) forecasts and the Department of Agricultural Extension (**DAE hotline 16123**).
8. **No soil chemistry from satellites**: NASA SMAP and POWER (`GWETTOP`, `GWETROOT`) indicate physical soil wetness only. They never measure soil pH, salinity (`EC`), organic matter, or NPK nutrient status. BoponX enforces `422 SOIL_TEST_REQUIRED` when a pH value is supplied without a lab soil test and `409 AGRONOMIC_RULES_NOT_APPROVED` on prescriptive rotation ranking.
9. **Preliminary agronomic thresholds**: Crop GDD targets (`crop_gdd_targets`) and numeric soil-wetness / water-balance task triggers (`GWETTOP < 35%` or `> 80%`, `GWETROOT < 40%`, `P − ET₀ < −2.0 mm/day`) are labelled `preliminary_estimate` or `rule_based` until cultivar- and AEZ-specific BARC/BRRI/BARI tables are signed off.
10. **Bangla copy**: Bangla strings in the advisory and 90-day planner carry `needs_native_bn_review: true` until reviewed by a native Bangla agricultural extension specialist.
